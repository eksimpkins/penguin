This is an unofficial Minecraft 1.21.11 compatibility rebuild of Minecoin's
CosmicUtilities 1.5, not an official Minecoin release. Original attribution,
assets and All Rights Reserved metadata are retained. Compatibility changes
include Minecraft rendering/API adaptations, the offscreen G Focus marker fix,
and a fix for errors while reloading saved cooldown settings. Revision 3 restores
Minecraft's normal mining and item-use checks and limits Peaceful Mining block
targeting to the player's normal block reach.
